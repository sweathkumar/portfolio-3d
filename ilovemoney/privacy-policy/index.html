I Love Money: Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-05 · App: I Love Money: Expense Tracker (com.sweathkumar.ilovemoney)

Summary

I Love Money has no server and no account system. Your financial data stays on your phone and isn't sold, shared or uploaded. The app has no internet permission.

What is stored

Transactions (amount, type, category, merchant, account ending, payment method, reference number, date), accounts, budgets, goals, balance updates from bank alerts, your settings, and the profile details you choose to enter (name, optional photo, gender, mobile number and age). All of it is stored only on your phone.

What is never stored

Full notification or SMS text, OTPs, passwords, PINs, CVVs and full card or account numbers. Security codes are detected and dropped before anything else is read.

Notification access

Used only for the banks, payment apps and SMS apps you select. Notifications from any other app are discarded immediately without being stored or logged. A short detection history (what was recognised, not the message text) is kept and cleared after the period you choose.

Display over other apps

Used only to show the transaction card when a payment is detected.

Google Sign-In (optional)

If you sign in, your Google name and email are stored on the phone to show in your profile. The sign-in token is used once on the phone and isn't stored or sent anywhere.

Profile photo

If you pick a photo, a cropped copy is saved in the app's private storage. The original stays in your gallery.

Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. Data leaves the phone only when you export a CSV or back up the database yourself, to a place you choose.

Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android encrypts and keeps closed to other apps. Cloud backup and device transfer are turned off. Optional protection levels can block screenshots and refuse to run on rooted phones.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, export your data as CSV, back up the database, or delete everything from Profile → Data backup → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also removes its data.

Children

The app isn't meant for anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the app shows the new version and asks you to agree again.
