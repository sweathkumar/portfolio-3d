01Introduction

The person behind the code.

I'm a creative developer and technology enthusiast who believes great ideas deserve to become meaningful experiences. I explore possibilities, take things apart to understand how they work, and turn complex ideas into practical solutions.

My work lives in software, but my interests travel much further: product thinking, creative design, artificial intelligence, entrepreneurship and the transportation systems that keep the world moving.

I don't just want to write code. I want to create products, experiences and ideas that make a difference.

02My Story

I have always been the person who asks how things work. That question pulled me into software, but it never stayed there. It led me into design, into product thinking, into artificial intelligence, into the way businesses grow and the way railway networks keep a country moving. I learn most things on my own, by taking them apart, getting them wrong and trying again until they are right. Today I work as a Software Development Engineer, but the title is only the part you can see. Underneath it is someone who wants to turn ideas into things people actually use.
